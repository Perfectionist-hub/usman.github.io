Architecture & Specification for Clinical RAG Chatbot

Academic AI Lab Project | Patient Education Bot

The Challenge: Patients researching plastic surgery often encounter dense medical terminology or unreliable information online.

The Solution: Designed a comprehensive specification for an educational chatbot that translates current journal literature and clinical guidelines into accessible language. The architecture utilizes a constrained LLM environment guided by an agent.md file to enforce strict safety behaviors and source restrictions.

  • Evidence-Based Grounding: Restricted knowledge retrieval strictly to major peer-reviewed journals and clinical guidelines, explicitly excluding unverified platforms like social media.
  • Clinical Guardrails: Programmed safety behavior to include medical disclaimers, refuse off-topic diagnostic queries, and suggest non-surgical alternatives to prevent bias.
  • Financial Transparency: Integrated specific logic rules for cost information, leveraging sources like the CMS Medicare Physician Fee Schedule while segregating financial data from clinical claims.
